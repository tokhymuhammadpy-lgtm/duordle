Privacy Policy

Duordle does not require an account. The only personal data collected is the username you type in each time you visit, which exists only for the duration of your browser session (held in memory on the server while you're connected, and in your browser's local storage so it's remembered on your own device) — it is never written to a database or shared with any third party.

Standard web server logs (IP address, timestamp, requested page) may be kept briefly for security and abuse-prevention purposes (see our bot-protection and rate-limiting measures) and are not used for tracking or advertising.

Duordle uses no advertising, no analytics trackers, and no third-party cookies.

Questions about this policy can be directed to the site owner.
